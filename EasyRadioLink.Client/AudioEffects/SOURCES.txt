Sound effect sources
====================

All effects used by the radio pipeline are 16-bit PCM, 48000 Hz, mono WAV files (anything else is ignored).
Most of them come from DCS-SimpleRadio Standalone (GPL-3.0), the project EasyRadioLink is based on.

RADIO_TRANS_START.wav, RADIO_TRANS_START_ALTERNATE.wav, RADIO_TRANS_END.wav, RADIO_TRANS_END_ALTERNATE.wav
    PTT tones and effects by "Bimbac" (shown as "Click" and "Soft click").

RADIO_TRANS_START_CHIRP.wav, RADIO_TRANS_START_KEY_UP_BEEP.wav, RADIO_TRANS_END_ROGER_BEEP.wav,
RADIO_TRANS_END_DOUBLE_BEEP.wav, RADIO_TRANS_END_THREE_TONE_BEEP.wav, BUSY_TONE.wav
    Generated by EasyRadioLink (synthesised sine tones, no third-party material) with tools/generate-sounds.py in
    the source repository, which reproduces them byte for byte. Levels matched to RADIO_TRANS_START.wav
    (-24 dBFS RMS). BUSY_TONE.wav (two 80 ms beeps at 440 Hz, 60 ms apart) is played only to the sender when
    push-to-talk is refused because another station is using the frequency (busy channel lockout).

FancyRelease.wav, AlmostFancy.wav
    Provided by the EasyRadioLink project owner (shown as "Fancy Release" and "Almost Fancy", selectable as start and
    as end sounds). Converted with tools/convert-sound.py to 16-bit PCM, 48000 Hz, mono with 2 ms fades and matched
    to the loudness of the other sounds (-24 dBFS RMS). The originals are kept in tools/sound-sources in the source
    repository and are not shipped.

NATO_TONE.wav
    FM background tone, from DCS-SimpleRadio Standalone.

SQUELCH_TAIL_AM.wav, SQUELCH_TAIL_FM.wav
    0.6 s excerpts (level normalised to -22 dBFS RMS) of the HF and FM noise recordings shipped with
    DCS-SimpleRadio Standalone.

Background\jet.wav, Background\prop.wav, Background\helicopter.wav
    Generic JET / PROP / HELICOPTER ambience from DCS-SimpleRadio Standalone.

beep-connected.wav, beep-disconnected.wav
    https://freesound.org/people/pan14/sounds/263124/ and https://freesound.org/people/pan14/sounds/263123/
    (taken 2018-10-03).
