EasyRadioLink 1.3.0: **voices that sound like a real radio link**. A new *Voice distortion* setting roughens the
received voice itself, not just the sound around it - and the squelch tail is now off by default. And like on a real
radio, **only one station can talk on a frequency at a time**.

## New

- **Voice distortion** (Settings > Radio Sounds, 0 - 100 %, default 35 %): received voices get a narrower band,
  overdrive (at the same loudness), lo-fi crunch and slow fading; above 50 % also crackle and short dropouts. Below
  30 MHz (HF, CB, MW) the fading is deeper, on the DIG band the voice breaks up digitally (short mutes and robotic
  repeats) instead. 35 % is rough but easy to understand, 100 % badly broken with the words still recognisable. It
  acts on the whole received voice at any radio effect strength (also on the clean share of that mix); it is not
  used on clean frequencies or when the radio effect strength is 0 %, and the squelch tail and the start / end sounds
  stay clean. The **Audio Preview** on the Radio tab uses it too, so you can tune it while you hear yourself.
- **Squelch tail off by default**: new profiles start without the "kssht" at the end of a transmission. Profiles that
  already have the setting keep it - switch it off under Settings > Radio Sounds > *Squelch tail*.
- **One speaker per frequency** (busy channel lockout, on by default): while somebody is talking on a frequency,
  nobody else can transmit on it - like real radios with busy channel lockout. The server decides: the first station
  to transmit holds the frequency until 0.3 s after its last transmission; voice of other stations on that frequency
  is not forwarded to anybody. On the radio a red **BUSY** is lit while another station uses your frequency. If you
  press push-to-talk then, you hear a short busy tone (two low beeps, only you hear it, played over the received voice
  without delaying it), BUSY flashes and nothing is sent for the whole press: release and press again once the
  frequency is free. If two stations press at the same moment, the first one gets through and the other one hears
  the busy tone and stops transmitting. With voice activation (VOX) nothing is sent while the frequency is busy (busy
  tone at most once per second). A press that sends nothing does not block your receiver on half-duplex servers.
  Radio check (echo) frequencies follow the same rule; your own echo never makes the frequency busy.
- Server: new setting *One speaker per frequency (busy channel lockout)* in the *Radio realism* group of the server
  window, `--busy-lockout` for the command-line server (`BUSY_CHANNEL_LOCKOUT` in `server.cfg`, default `true`). A
  change applies at once. **Server Info** in the client shows whether it is on.

## Notes

- One speaker per frequency needs no protocol change: 1.3 servers and clients work with 1.1 / 1.2 ones. A 1.1 / 1.2
  client on a 1.3 server is locked out like everybody else, but it shows no BUSY and plays no busy tone - it just
  isn't heard while the frequency is busy. A 1.3 client on an older server shows the setting as off and transmits as
  before (older servers lock nothing out). To keep the old behaviour (everybody may talk at the same time, e.g. with
  *Radio interference*), switch the setting off on the server.
- The voice distortion is applied by each listener, like the other radio effects: everybody hears the others with
  their own setting. Recordings of received audio contain it as well; your own voice (mic output device, recording
  of your own transmissions) does not.
