EasyRadioLink 1.2.0: **clearer settings and your own radio sounds**. Choose which sound plays when you press and release
push-to-talk and when someone else starts and stops talking - with a play button to listen. Everything else is tidied
away under *Advanced settings*.

1.2 works together with 1.1: clients and servers can be updated one after the other.

## Downloads

| File | Who needs it |
|---|---|
| `EasyRadioLink-Client-1.2.0.zip` | Everybody who wants to talk. Extract it and start `EasyRadioLink.exe`. |
| `EasyRadioLink-Server-1.2.0-Windows.zip` | Only whoever hosts a server on Windows: `Server\EasyRadioLink.Server.exe` (server with window) and `CommandLine\EasyRadioLink.Server.Cli.exe` (command-line server). |
| `EasyRadioLink-Server-1.2.0-Linux.tar.gz` | Only whoever hosts a server on Linux (x64): `EasyRadioLink.Server.Cli` and `easyradiolink.service` (hardened systemd service). |

The requirements are the same as for 1.1 (see `README.txt` in the download).

## New

- **Settings in two parts**: at the top the things you actually change - *Radio Sounds* and *General* (open the radio
  when connected, show who is transmitting, tray, connection sounds, voice activation). Everything else is in
  **Advanced settings**, collapsed until you open it: microphone and incoming audio processing, voice activation
  details, recording, radio effect details, push-to-talk and controllers, radio window, profiles. EasyRadioLink
  remembers whether it is open.
- **Radio sounds of your choice** for four moments: *When I press push-to-talk*, *When I release push-to-talk*,
  *When someone starts talking* and *When someone stops talking* - each with *Off* and a ▶ button that plays the sound
  on your speakers, also without a connection. The sounds for other people starting and stopping are separate
  settings now (before, they were your own push-to-talk sounds).
- **New sounds**: *Fancy Release* and *Almost Fancy* (for the start and the end), the classic CB *Roger beep*, a
  *Double beep*, a *Three-tone beep*, a *Chirp* and a *Key-up beep*, next to *Click* and *Soft click*.
- **New defaults**: *Fancy Release* when a transmission starts and *Almost Fancy* when it ends. Profiles that already
  have their own choice keep it.

## Notes

- Only you hear your push-to-talk sounds; the others hear the sounds they chose for someone starting / stopping.
- The start sound of a received transmission plays before the voice, so a longer sound delays the voice by its length
  (*Fancy Release*: about 0.3 s).
- Own sounds: `tools/convert-sound.py` in the source repository turns any WAV into the format EasyRadioLink loads and
  matches the loudness of the other sounds (see *Building from source* in the README).
